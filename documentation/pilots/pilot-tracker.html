How pilots are judged

  • pass@1 should rise toward pass@8. That is RL turning "sometimes right" into "right first time".
  • pass@8 should not fall by more than noise. A question-by-question sign test against the baseline separates a real drop from chance. A drop means the method is narrowing the model's answers, not teaching it.
  • The canaries stay steady: entropy, KL to the SFT model, thought length and the share of well-formed calls. A pilot that drifts is stopped and reported.

The reading column in the table is a rough guide from these rules. The decision is made in the written tracker, not here.
