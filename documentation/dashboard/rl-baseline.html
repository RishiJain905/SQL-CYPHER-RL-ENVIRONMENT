How to read this

pass@1 is the share of questions answered right in a single try. Greedy is the model's one most likely answer, the score reported so far; mean@8 is the average over the 8 sampled answers, and a mean far below greedy would mean temperature 1.0 is too wild to train on.

pass@8 counts a question as solved when any of its 8 answers is right, so the gap between pass@8 and greedy is roughly what RL can gain.

Each question lands in one bucket. Right 0 of 8 gives GRPO nothing to learn from. Right 1 to 7 of 8 is GRPO's learning pool, because it can reward the right answers over their wrong siblings. Right 8 of 8 is already solved. A ringed cell in the grid is an easy win: the greedy answer was wrong, yet at least one sample was right.
